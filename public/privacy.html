FinTrac Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

FinTrac is a personal finance tracker for recording income and expenses, bills, savings jars and savings challenges. This policy explains what information FinTrac collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we also receive your Google profile photo link.
  • Profile details you choose to add: birthday, gender, occupation and an avatar. A photo you pick from your gallery stays on your device; only its on-device location is saved to your profile.
  • Financial records you enter: transactions (amount, category, description, date), bills (name, amount, category, due date, paid status), savings jars (name, goal, amount saved, deadline, members and their contributions) and savings challenges (target, progress, check-in times).
  • Notifications the app creates for you, such as bill reminders and jar invitations.
  • App diagnostics: crash reports and basic usage statistics, used to fix problems and improve the app.

FinTrac does not access your bank accounts, cards, contacts, location or messages.

How we use it

  • To provide the app's features: storing your records, calculating balances and reports, and syncing across your devices.
  • To verify your email address with a one-time code when you sign up and log in.
  • To send reminders you have not turned off (you can turn them off in Settings).
  • To diagnose crashes and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • You. Your transactions, bills, challenges and notifications are visible only to your account.
  • Members of a shared jar. If you join a shared jar, its members can see your name, profile avatar, your contributions to that jar and the jar's activity.
  • Service providers that run parts of FinTrac for us:
    • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics, App Check) stores and processes app data. Firebase privacy information.
    • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
    • EmailJS receives your email address and the one-time code in order to send the verification email. EmailJS privacy policy.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored in Google's cloud. Database access rules limit each account to its own data and to the shared jars it belongs to. Data stored on your phone by the app is excluded from device backups.

How long we keep it

Your information is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Items you archive (jars, bills, challenges) are deleted automatically 30 days after archiving.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Settings, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. After confirming your identity, FinTrac deletes your account, your profile, transactions, bills, challenges, notifications, the jars you own (with their activity) and your entries in other jars' activity, and removes you from jars others own. This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app: email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We will delete it and all associated data within 30 days.

Crash reports and usage statistics are kept by Firebase for a limited period as described in its privacy information and are not linked to your financial records.

Children

FinTrac is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
